Getting started

I can't find the Credara button in Chrome

Chrome hides new extensions behind the puzzle-piece icon at the top right.

  1. Click the puzzle piece 🧩 next to the address bar.
  2. Find Credara and click the pin next to it.

The Credara button now stays in the toolbar. You use it to capture jobs, capture emails and fill in application forms.

How do I get the latest version?

You don't need to do anything. Chrome updates Credara by itself, usually within a few hours of a new version. To get it sooner, close Chrome completely and open it again.

To see which version you have, open chrome://extensions in the address bar and look at the Credara card.

Never click Remove to "reinstall" Credara: that deletes the data stored in Chrome. If you're signed in, it's also saved online, but export a backup first to be safe (see Backing up).

What should I do first?

Home shows a short getting-started list. In order: sign in (Settings → Online account), import your résumés, build your career profile, choose a look, and fill in your application details. Then capture your first job.

Signing in & your data

The sign-in link doesn't work

Credara signs you in with a link sent by email, from signin@credara.app. The link only works when:

  • you open it in the same Chrome, on the same computer, where you asked for it,
  • it's less than 2 hours old, and
  • it hasn't been used already.

If it fails, go to Settings → Online account and ask for a new link. Can't find the email? Check your spam or promotions folder.

Can I use Credara on a second computer?

Yes. Install Credara on the other computer and sign in with the same email address. Your jobs, résumés and profile download automatically. After that, changes are saved online and appear on both computers.

If you changed something on both computers before they could catch up, Credara asks which version to keep.

Backing up my data

When you're signed in, Credara saves your data online as you work. You can also keep a file copy:

  1. Go to Settings → Your data.
  2. Click Export my data. A file is saved to your Downloads folder.
  3. To restore it, use Import my data on the same card and choose that file.

Export a backup before reinstalling Chrome or moving to a new computer without signing in.

What happens if I sign out?

Your data stays in Chrome on that computer, and stays saved online. If someone else signs in to Credara on the same computer, their data is kept separate from yours.

Résumés & files

My downloaded résumé opened in Notepad (or the PDF opened in Word)

The file is fine. Windows is opening it with the wrong program. To fix it once:

  1. Right-click the file in your Downloads folder.
  2. Choose Open with → Choose another app.
  3. Pick Word for .docx files (or your PDF reader, such as Edge or Acrobat, for .pdf files), tick Always use this app, and click OK.
Should I add more than one résumé?

Yes, if you have them. Each version you've written adds wording Credara can choose from, and your career profile combines them into one list of everything you've done. When you build a résumé for a job, Credara picks the bullets that fit that job best.

Can I use my own Word layout instead of Credara's?

Yes. Credara's own formats (Classic, Modern, Compact, under Résumé → Look) are the default. On a job's résumé step, choose Use my own Word layout to work from one of your Word files instead.

My résumé is too long

When you build a résumé for a job, a page meter shows how long it is. Credara aims for 2 pages (1 page for the Compact format) and trims the bullets that matter least for that job first. You can untick more bullets yourself, or switch to Compact under Résumé → Look.

Jobs

Capturing a job missed the company or the description

Some job sites hide parts of the page. Credara tells you on the check screen when something is missing. Type the company in, and paste the description into the box. Everything can also be changed later on the job's Overview page.

A job came through a contact, with no posting. Is that a problem?

No. You can track it, prepare for interviews and log messages without a posting. To build a résumé or cover letter for it, Credara needs something about the role: paste the role description, or a few lines of what your contact told you, into the box on the job's Overview page. You can replace it when the full spec arrives.

If the contact emailed you, use Capture email on the Credara button: Credara saves what they wrote about the role for you.

Can I bring in jobs I already track in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Go to Jobs → Import from a spreadsheet and choose an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file. In Google Sheets, first use File → Download → Microsoft Excel. You check every job before anything is saved. If your layout doesn't read well, download the Credara template from the same card.

Credara didn't read the date from my interview email

Open the email itself (not the inbox list) before clicking Capture email. With calendar invitations, open the invitation so its date and time are on screen. If a detail is still missing, Credara leaves it blank for you to fill in rather than guessing.

Applying

Credara didn't fill in some fields

Credara fills only what it's sure of, from your Application details. Anything uncertain, like a dropdown with two similar choices, is left for you. New questions it couldn't answer appear under Application details → To answer, so next time it can fill them.

Voluntary questions (gender, race, disability, veteran status) are never filled in for you.

My résumé didn't attach to the employer's form

In the Credara button, check that the right job is selected above the upload buttons. If the site still won't accept it, use Save … to drag in in the Credara button: Credara saves the exact same file, and you drag it onto the employer's upload box.

▶ Watch: Applying on the employer's site
Does Credara submit applications for me?

No, never. Credara prepares everything and can fill in the form, but you always check it and press Submit yourself. Afterwards, mark the job as Applied in Credara.

AI actions

Which AI does Credara use?

Credara uses Claude, made by Anthropic. Your request goes from Credara's own server to Anthropic's API — you don't need an account with Anthropic, and nothing is sent under your name.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, what Credara sends is not used to train its models. Credara's servers keep only counts and errors, not your résumé or documents. More detail is in the privacy policy.

Whatever the AI suggests, Credara checks it against your own verified experience before you see it, and you approve every change.

What is the daily limit of AI actions?

Each person can use 30 AI actions a day. The limit resets the next day.

These count: building a résumé for a job, writing a cover letter, interview prep, mock-interview feedback, reading a captured email, a résumé file or a photo of your notes, suggesting roles or related titles, Help me write it, checking for similar bullets, and today's top 5.

These don't: tracking jobs, editing, downloading files, importing a spreadsheet, filling in forms, dictating notes, and anything you type yourself.

An AI step said it failed or is busy

Wait a minute and try again. A failed attempt doesn't change anything you've saved. If it keeps happening, use Send feedback in the app and say which step it was.

Honesty & privacy

Will Credara make up experience to fit a job?

No. Credara only chooses and orders your own bullet points, under the employer where the work happened. It never invents experience, numbers or credentials. When a job asks for something you haven't written down, Credara points out the gap and can help you write a bullet in your own words, which you confirm is accurate before it's used.

Can I see everything before it goes to an employer?

Yes. What you approve on screen is exactly what's in the file you download or upload. If Credara can't put an approved change into your file, it stops and tells you why instead of producing a different file.

Who can see my data?

Your data is private to your account. Credara never stores your passwords for job sites. Read the full privacy policy.
