Credara

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Credara is a browser extension that helps you prepare job applications. This policy explains what information Credara handles, where it goes and the choices you have. In short: your information is used only to run Credara for you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and Credara contains no tracking or analytics.

What Credara stores

  • Your account: the email address you sign in with. Sign-in works by an emailed link; Credara has no passwords and never stores passwords for job sites.
  • What you add: résumés you import (their text and, for Word files, the file itself), bullets you write, your application profile (for example name, contact details, location, education and standard answers to application questions), jobs you save, cover letters, interview rounds and notes, people you add to a job, and correspondence you log.
  • Feedback you send: if you use Send feedback, Credara stores what you type, whether it is an idea or a problem, whether we may email you about it, the Credara page you were on and the version number, linked to your account so we can reply. It never includes your résumé or jobs. Only the Credara team can read it.
  • Where it is kept: in your browser's extension storage on your computer and, when you are signed in, in your Credara account so it is available on your other computers. The account database is hosted by Supabase in the United States and is set up so only your signed-in account can read or change your data. The last 50 saved versions of your data are kept so a mistake can be undone.

Pages you visit

Credara reads a web page only when you click one of its buttons on that page: Capture this job (reads the job posting), Capture email (reads the email you have open, or the text you selected — for example an interview invite, or a recruiter or contact telling you about a role) and Fill what Credara knows (reads the questions on the application form you are on and fills only answers you have reviewed). Credara does not watch or record your browsing. Sensitive questions such as gender, race, ethnicity, disability or veteran status are never inferred and never filled for you.

AI features

When you use an AI feature (job fit analysis, résumé suggestions, cover letters, interview preparation, mock interview feedback, message drafts, résumé import, reading a photo of your notes or the daily job picks), the information that feature needs is sent from Credara's server to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the result. For example, a cover letter request includes the job posting and your verified experience. Anthropic processes it to produce the answer; under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models. Photos of notes are read and then discarded; Credara does not keep the image. Credara's servers log only counts and errors, not the content of your résumé or documents. Each account has a daily limit on AI actions; Credara keeps a count of actions per day to enforce it.

Other services Credara uses

  • Supabase stores your account data and runs Credara's server functions.
  • Anthropic provides the AI described above.
  • Resend sends your sign-in emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Google speech recognition: if you use 🎤 Dictate, Chrome's built-in speech recognition sends the audio to Google to turn it into text. Dictation is optional.
  • Public job boards: the daily job search reads public job listings from company career sites. It sends none of your information to those sites.

Credara does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and does not use it for advertising or to decide creditworthiness or lending.

Your choices

  • Download your data at any time with Export my data on the Profile page.
  • Feedback: sending feedback is optional. If you are not signed in, Send feedback opens an email to us instead.
  • Delete items such as jobs, interview rounds, people and résumés inside Credara.
  • Delete your account: email hello@credara.app from your sign-in address and we will delete your account and all of its online data, including any feedback you sent, within 30 days. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes the copy stored on that computer.
  • Use it without an account: without signing in, your data stays only on your computer, and AI features are not available.

Security

Connections to Credara's servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to account data is limited to your own signed-in account by database rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your email account secure, since it is how you sign in.

Children

Credara is meant for adults looking for work and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: hello@credara.app.
