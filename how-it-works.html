Getting started

2:03 · video 1 of 4

Sign in, add your résumés, build your career profile, choose a look, and fill in the details employers ask for.

Read the transcript

Getting started with Credara. In about two minutes you will sign in, add your résumés, and set up the details employers ask for.

Credara prepares each application with you: a résumé built for the job, a cover letter when one is needed, and help on the employer's form. You always review everything and press Submit yourself.

First, sign in. Open Settings and enter your email address. Credara emails you a sign-in link, so there is no password. Signing in saves your work online and turns on the AI features.

Next, add your résumés. Under Résumé, open My Word files and choose Import résumé. If you keep several versions, for different kinds of roles, add them all, in Word or PDF. Credara sorts each one into jobs and bullets, in your own words, and you confirm every line.

Then open My experience and choose Build my profile. Credara combines every version into one career profile: the same job is merged, and every bullet you have ever written for it is kept. From then on, Credara builds a résumé for each role from all of it.

Check each job and bullet. Untick anything you do not want to use, confirm that it is accurate, and save.

This is your career profile. You can edit a bullet, add one, or let Credara ask you a few questions and draft one for you. It only ever uses what you confirm is true, and it never moves experience to another employer.

Under Look, choose a style, a font and the paper size. Every résumé Credara builds for you uses this look, in Word and in PDF.

Finally, Application details holds what employer forms usually ask: your contact details, work eligibility and education. Use my résumé details fills in the empty boxes. Check them, answer the rest, and press Save.

You are set up. In the next video, you will capture a job and build a résumé made for it.

A résumé for one job

1:38 · video 2 of 4

Capture a job posting and let Credara build a résumé for that role from your own experience. Add a cover letter if the job asks for one.

Read the transcript

In this video, you will capture a job posting, and Credara will build a résumé made for that role, from your own experience.

Find a job you like, on a job board or a company's careers page. Click the Credara button in Chrome's toolbar, then Capture this job.

Credara reads the title, company, location and the full description. Check that it got them right, then choose Save and build my résumé.

Every job follows the same steps, shown along the top: your résumé, an optional cover letter, review, and apply. After you apply, the same page tracks interviews and follow-ups.

Choose Build my résumé. Credara reads what the job asks for, and picks your most relevant experience for it, from everything in your profile, across every version of your résumé. It never rewrites your bullets or moves them to another employer.

You stay in charge. Tick or untick any bullet, and the page meter keeps the résumé to your target length. Gaps the job asks about are listed too, with help to add a bullet if you have the experience. When it looks right, save it.

Next, the cover letter. Many jobs do not ask for one, so you can skip it. If you want one, Credara drafts it from your verified experience, and shows where every sentence comes from. Edit it as you like.

Finally, review. Download your résumé and cover letter as Word or PDF. The files are named with your name and the firm, ready to upload.

Next video: applying on the employer's site, with the Credara button.

Applying on the employer's site

1:25 · video 3 of 4

The Credara button attaches your résumé and fills in what it knows. You check the form and press Submit yourself.

Read the transcript

In this video, you will apply on the employer's own site, with the Credara button doing the typing and attaching. You always press Submit yourself.

When your résumé is ready, open the job's Review and apply step and choose Continue to employer application. Credara gets your documents ready for the Credara button.

On the employer's application form, click the Credara button. Under Applying on this page, it shows which job's documents it will use. It matches the page to the job, and if it cannot tell, it asks you to choose.

Choose Attach my résumé. The résumé built for this job goes straight into the résumé box, and Credara checks the page shows it. If a site will not take it, you can save the file and drag it in.

Choose Fill what Credara knows. You see every answer before anything is typed, from your Application details. Voluntary questions, like gender, are never filled; they stay with you.

Check the form, answer anything that is left, and press Submit yourself. Credara never submits an application for you.

Then come back to Credara and choose I submitted it, mark as Applied. Credara keeps a copy of exactly what you sent, and the job moves on to interviews and follow-ups.

Next video: what to do after you apply, from the first interview email to the thank-you note.

After you apply

1:37 · video 4 of 4

Interview emails, preparation, a mock interview, thank-you notes and follow-ups, all on the job's own page.

Read the transcript

In this last video: what happens after you apply. Interview emails, preparation, a mock interview and a thank-you note, all on the job's own page.

Once you have applied, the job opens on what happens next. When you hear back, add the email: paste it here, or open it in your webmail, like Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or AOL, and use Capture email on the Credara button.

Credara reads the date, the time, who you will meet, and the meeting link. Anything it cannot find in the email is left blank for you. Check it, then add the interview round.

Choose Prepare with AI for likely questions, talking points from your own experience, and honest answers for anything the job asks that you have not done yet. It is your notes page, so add to it freely.

Practise with a mock interview. Answer out loud or by typing, and get scores with reasons, what to fix, and an outline of a stronger answer. Try a question again, and the coach checks whether you applied its advice.

After each round, jot down your notes, by typing, dictating or attaching a photo. Credara drafts a thank-you note from them. Copy it or open it in your email, and send it yourself.

People keeps everyone you meet for this job, added from your emails. Draft a follow-up or a referral request, and Credara reminds you when it is time to follow up.

That is Credara. It prepares each application from your own experience, and you decide what goes out. If anything is unclear, use Send feedback in the app.
